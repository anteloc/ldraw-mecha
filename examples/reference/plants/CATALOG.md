# Plant wind catalog

Twenty LEGO plant cases moved by one shared wind, three wind clips each, in one scene of one file: [`plants.blend`](plants.blend) (scene **Wind**). Generated by `scripts/build_plants.py catalog` from `scripts/wind_clips.py`. See [`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md) for how the cases were built and checked.

## How to find a how-to

1. Pick a case by plant (table below) or a motion style (style index), or a joint kind (real LEGO freedom or `flex_approx`).
2. In `plants.blend` open the collection `Pxx_key Title` (outliner: *Plant wind catalog*). It holds the model, its rig, the controller `CTRL_plant_xx_*` with the three NLA tracks, the floor labels and the camera `CAM_Pxx`; set `CAM_Pxx` as the active camera to see that case alone, or keep `CAM_Overview` for the field.
3. Reuse the clip's Action (Blender), its JSON values (`clips/<case>.<regime>.json`), or regenerate it with the same response recipe for a new model (see *Reusing a wind response*).

Timeline (24 fps): **A breeze** frames 1–240, **B gusty** 257–496, **C storm** 513–752 (markers of the same names). Every clip starts and ends at rest (spins on a symmetric step); between windows each plant holds its rest pose. Breeze clips loop seamlessly over clip frames 25–217 (timeline 25–217).

## The wind

One wind blows toward **+X** (left to right in the overview, LDraw and Blender X). Its speed U and cross component V are normalised (storm gusts reach about 1.1). Gusts and turbulence are frozen into the air and travel along +X at 48 LDU per frame from x = -1280 LDU, so a case in column c feels every gust later than the column to its left:

| Column | Slot x (LDU) | Delay (frames) | Cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | -1040 | 5.0 | P01, P08, P14, P18 |
| 2 | -520 | 15.8 | P02, P04, P10, P19 |
| 3 | 0 | 26.7 | P03, P07, P11, P13 |
| 4 | 520 | 37.5 | P05, P06, P09, P16 |
| 5 | 1040 | 48.3 | P12, P15, P17, P20 |

| Regime | Mean U | Gusts (clip frame: extra U) | Turbulence / cross wind | Envelope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A breeze | 0.42 | — (periodic swell, 96-frame period) | ±0.15 / ±0.09 | constant (clip faded in/out over 24 frames) |
| B gusty | 0.45 | 48: +0.33, 104: +0.42, 150: +0.3 | ±0.10 / ±0.13 | rises over frames 0–30, dies over 145–175 |
| C storm | 0.8 | 40: +0.22, 88: +0.3, 126: +0.2, 158: +0.28 | ±0.16 / ±0.28 | rises over frames 0–36, dies over 150–180 |

The **WIND** object (collection *Wind*) carries the same field at x = 0 as custom properties `speed`, `u`, `v` on its own NLA tracks (Actions `WIND.breeze`, `WIND.gusty`, `WIND.storm`) and drives the length of the blue arrow. It is a reference signal: the plant clips were computed from the field directly.

## Reusing a wind response

- **Data only:** `clips/<case>.<regime>.json` holds one value per frame (`baked`), each channel's unit, range, meaning, joint kind, response recipe and wind delay. `clips/WIND.json` holds the shared wind.
- **Blender:** every clip is an Action with one slot (`plant_controller`) keyed on the controller's custom properties (`["bend_1"]`, `["frond_upwind"]` …), one LINEAR key per frame. It plays on any controller with the same property names and meanings:

```python
import bpy
src = '/abs/path/output/plants/plants.blend'
with bpy.data.libraries.load(src, link=False) as (data_from, data_to):
    data_to.actions = ['P02_swordleaf_palm.storm']
action = data_to.actions[0]
ctrl = bpy.data.objects['CTRL_plant_13_fern']  # any controller with frond_downwind / frond_upwind / ...
ad = ctrl.animation_data_create()
track = ad.nla_tracks.new()
strip = track.strips.new(action.name, 1, action)
strip.action_slot = action.slots[0]
```

- **Whole case:** append the collection `Pxx_key Title` (model, rig, controller with its NLA tracks, SLOT_ empty, camera); the Actions come along through the NLA strips.
- **A new plant on another model:** rig the joint (same kind of connection; measure axis and origin), choose the + sign by the conventions above, then generate its clips from a recipe of the closest case. The recipe's `x` (joint x) and `swing` (horizontal motion of the tip per radian) must be remeasured on the new model; they decide how the shared wind direction acts on the joint:

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, 'scripts')
import wind_clips as wc
spec = wc.control('radian', -0.3, 0.3, 'Branch on its clip; + lifts the tip',
                  wc.osc(1.3, 0.05, 0.9, x=0.0, swing=(0.12, 0.0), lift=0.2))  # as P11 branch_bounce
breeze = wc.respond(spec, 'A', slot_x=0.0)   # 240 values, loops over frames 25-217
storm = wc.respond(spec, 'C', slot_x=0.0)
```

Recipes: `osc` (damped second-order response to drag; `lag` delays it, `lift` adds a rise in strong wind, `threshold` ignores weak wind, `flutter` adds fast sines growing with |W|²), `spin` (a rotor whose speed follows the wind), `vane` (turns toward the downwind heading). A chain (whip) uses one `osc` per joint with growing gain and lag toward the tip.
